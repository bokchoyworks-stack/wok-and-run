FAQ

How do I restore purchases?

Open the gear icon in the game, then choose Restore Purchase. Use the same Apple Account that made the original purchase.

What does the Full Game purchase include?

It unlocks stages 4–20. Ads may still appear only on the Continue screen unless Remove Ads is also purchased.

What does Remove Ads include?

It permanently removes the banner shown on the Continue screen. It does not unlock stages 4–20.

Where are ads shown?

Only on the 15-second Continue screen after five misses. Ads do not cover active gameplay.

How do I change the language?

Open the gear icon and choose English, 日本語, or 简体中文.

My purchase is not appearing.

Check your internet connection, confirm you are signed into the correct Apple Account, then use Restore Purchase. If it still does not appear, contact support.
