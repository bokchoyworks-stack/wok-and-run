Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Wok and Run handles information.

Effective: September 21, 2026 · Developer: Hiroki Murai / Bokchoy Works

Summary: No account is required. Progress stays on the device. The ad-supported version uses child-directed, non-personalized Google AdMob.

1. Information handled by the app

Wok and Run does not require an account and the developer does not directly collect your name, email address, precise location, contacts, photos, or game content. Game progress, language, sound, and other settings are stored locally on your device.

2. Advertising and Google AdMob

The ad-supported version uses Google AdMob. Ads appear only on the 15-second Continue screen after five misses. The app configures ads as child-directed, limits ad content to the General audience rating, and disables personalized advertising. Google Mobile Ads may automatically process an IP address (which can estimate general location), device identifiers, advertising data, product interactions, crash logs, and performance data for ad delivery, fraud prevention, analytics, and SDK operation. Wok and Run does not request App Tracking Transparency permission and does not use this data to build a developer-controlled profile.

3. Purchases

One-time purchases for unlocking stages and removing ads are processed by Apple through StoreKit. The developer receives transaction entitlement status but does not receive your payment card details.

4. Children

The app is configured for child-directed treatment. We do not knowingly ask children to submit personal information. Purchases and external links are placed behind a parental gate.

5. Privacy choices

Where required, privacy choices are available from the in-game Settings screen. You can also manage device privacy settings in iOS. Purchasing Remove Ads stops AdMob banners from appearing in the app.

6. Retention and sharing

The developer does not operate a server that stores user profiles or gameplay histories. Google and Apple retain and process information according to their own policies. Information may be disclosed when required by law or to protect users and the service.

7. International processing

Google and Apple may process information in countries or regions other than where you live, subject to their safeguards and applicable law.

8. Changes and contact

We may update this policy when the app or applicable requirements change. The current version and effective date will remain posted here. For privacy questions, contact us through the support page.
